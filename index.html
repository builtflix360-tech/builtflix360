About BuiltFlix 360

Builders who still answer the phone on day 40

We started in 2009 with one van, two bricklayers and a rule we have never dropped: finish what you start, and leave the place cleaner than you found it.

Sixteen years later we are a team of 34 covering everything from a leaking radiator to a two-storey rear extension. Large enough to hold a build programme together, small enough that the person who prices your job is the person you call when you have a question about it.

Every project gets one named project manager, a written schedule you can hold us to, and a price that does not move unless you ask for something new. That is the whole business model.

In London, single storey extensions generally land between £2,200 and £3,000 per square metre depending on structure, glazing and finish level. A side return typically runs £55,000 to £90,000 all in. We give you a fixed figure after the site visit rather than a range.

Many extensions and loft conversions fall under permitted development, which means no planning application. We check your property against the current rules at the site visit and tell you straight away which route you are on. If you do need permission, we prepare and submit the application for you.

A bathroom takes seven to ten working days, a kitchen two to three weeks, a loft conversion six to nine weeks and a single storey extension ten to sixteen weeks. You get a week-by-week programme with your quote, and a written update every Friday while we are on site.

Yes. The price only changes if you ask for something that was not in the specification, or if we open up a floor and find something genuinely hidden, such as failed drainage. In that case you get a written variation with costs before any extra work starts. Nothing is added after the fact.

For extensions, loft conversions and single-room renovations, almost always. We set up dust screens, protect floors and routes, and keep at least one bathroom and the kitchen usable wherever possible. For full refurbishments with a rewire, most clients move out for two to three weeks.

Payments are tied to completed stages, never to time passing. Typically a deposit to secure materials and dates, then stage payments as agreed milestones are signed off, with the final balance due after the handover walkaround and snag list are complete.
