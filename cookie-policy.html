Cookie policy

Last updated: 27 September 2026

This policy explains how cookies and similar technologies are used when you visit builtflix360.co.uk.

1. What cookies are

Cookies are small text files that a website saves on your device. They are widely used to make websites work, to remember preferences and to collect information about how a site is used.

2. Cookies set by our website

Our website does not set any cookies of its own. We do not use analytics, advertising or tracking cookies, and we do not store information on your device for marketing.

3. Third-party services

Some parts of the website are provided by other companies. When your browser loads them, those companies may receive technical information such as your IP address, and in some cases may set cookies.

ServiceWhere it is usedWhat happens
Google MapsThe map in the Contact sectionWhen the map loads, Google receives your IP address and may set cookies on your device. This is covered by Google’s privacy policy and cookie information.
Google FontsEvery page, to display our typefacesYour browser requests the font files from Google, which receives your IP address. Google Fonts does not set cookies.
WhatsApp and InstagramOnly if you click a WhatsApp or Instagram linkYou leave our website and that service’s own cookie and privacy policies apply.

4. Managing cookies

You can block or delete cookies, including those set by Google Maps, in your browser settings. Most browsers also let you block third-party cookies only. Blocking cookies will not stop you using our website or sending us a quote request, although the map may not display.

Guides for common browsers: Chrome, Safari, Firefox, Edge.
