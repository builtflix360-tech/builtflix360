Privacy policy

Last updated: 27 September 2026

This policy explains how BuiltFlix 360 (“we”, “us”, “our”) collects and uses personal information when you visit builtflix360.co.uk, ask us for a quote or otherwise contact us. We handle your information in line with the UK General Data Protection Regulation (UK GDPR) and the Data Protection Act 2018.

BuiltFlix 360 is the data controller for the personal information described in this policy. You can contact us about anything in this policy using the details below.

2. Information we collect

Information you give us

When you use the quote forms on our website, we collect:

  • your name, phone number and email address;
  • the postcode of the property;
  • the type of work you need, your rough budget and the best time to call you;
  • any project details you choose to write in the message box.

If you phone, email or message us on WhatsApp, we collect the information you choose to share, such as your contact details, photos of the property and details of the work. If you become a customer, we also hold the information needed to carry out the work, such as the property address, site access arrangements, quotes, invoices and payment records.

Information collected automatically

Like most websites, our hosting provider records basic technical information when you visit, such as your IP address, browser type and the pages requested. This is used to keep the website secure and working properly. We do not use analytics or advertising tracking on our website. See our Cookie policy for more detail.

4. Who we share your information with

We only share personal information where it is needed for the purposes above:

  • Hostinger, which hosts our website and email, and so processes quote form submissions and emails on our behalf;
  • Google, when you view the embedded map or when your browser loads the fonts used on our website (see our Cookie policy);
  • WhatsApp (Meta), if you choose to contact us through WhatsApp;
  • trades, suppliers, surveyors, building control and certification bodies where this is needed to carry out work you have agreed with us;
  • our accountants, insurers and professional advisers, and public authorities where we are required by law.

Some of these providers may process data outside the UK. Where they do, they are required to protect it with appropriate safeguards, such as UK adequacy regulations or approved contract terms.

5. How long we keep your information

  • Enquiries that do not go ahead: up to 12 months after our last contact, so we can pick up the conversation if you come back to us.
  • Customer and project records: 6 years after the work is completed, to meet tax and accounting rules. Records relating to guarantees, certificates or building regulations may be kept for as long as they could be needed to deal with a claim.

After these periods we delete or securely destroy the information.

6. Your rights

Under UK data protection law you have the right to:

  • ask for a copy of the personal information we hold about you;
  • ask us to correct information that is wrong or incomplete;
  • ask us to delete your information;
  • ask us to restrict how we use your information, or object to how we use it;
  • ask us to transfer information you have given us to another organisation.

Some of these rights have limits, for example where we must keep records by law. To use any of these rights, contact us at info@builtflix360.co.uk. We will respond within one month and there is normally no charge.

7. Keeping your information secure

Our website uses an encrypted (HTTPS) connection, access to our email and records is password protected, and we only give access to people who need it to do their job.

8. Complaints

If you are unhappy with how we have handled your information, please contact us first so we can try to put it right. You also have the right to complain to the Information Commissioner’s Office (ICO), the UK’s data protection regulator, at ico.org.uk/make-a-complaint or on 0303 123 1113.

9. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy from time to time. The latest version will always be on this page, with the date it was last updated shown at the top.
